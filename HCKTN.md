## The manifesto

- **Commit to main.** CI is the seatbelt; velocity is the engine.
- **The demo is the product.** If it can't be shown, it doesn't exist.
- **One working feature beats three almost-working ones.**
- **Vowels are optional.**
- **Fake it 'til the demo works.** Hardcode proudly — nobody audits auth.
- **Cut scope, not corners.** A small polished thing beats a big broken one.
- **The wow is in the last 10%.** Leave time for it.

Built in a weekend. Judged in three minutes. Worth it.
